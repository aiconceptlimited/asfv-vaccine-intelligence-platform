## Study Design
- PAMs from 3 pigs with different SLA-I haplotypes
- SLA-1, SLA-2, SLA-3 typed
- Infected with virulent BA71 or attenuated BA71ΔCD2 ASFV
- SLA-I immunoprecipitation + LC-MS/MS
- 132 SLA-I-bound peptide observations
- 106 distinct ASFV sequences from 56 ASFV proteins
- IFN-γ ELISpot validation of immunopeptidomic peptides

## Key Findings
- 5 immunopeptidomically identified peptides recognized by ASFV-specific T cells
- ASFV proteins identified: CP2475L/pp220, B646L/p72, B475L, D1133L, K145R, MGF proteins
