## Study Objective
To establish immortalized porcine alveolar macrophage cell lines with defined SLA-II haplotypes and test peptide binding.

## Key Limitation
The experiment measures peptide binding to the cellular SLA-II repertoire, not an isolated SLA-DRB1*02:01 molecule. The difference between haplotypes likely reflects allelic differences among the SLA-II molecules expressed.
