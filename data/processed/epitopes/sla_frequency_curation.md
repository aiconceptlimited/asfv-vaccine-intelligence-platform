# SLA POPULATION FREQUENCY CURATION

## Studies Identified

### 1. Kenyan Free-Ranging Pigs (2017)
- **Population:** South-West Kenyan pigs
- **Sample size:** 10 pigs
- **Loci:** SLA-1, SLA-2, SLA-3
- **Data:** Alleles, haplotypes, prevalence
- **Source:** Frontiers in Immunology, 2017
- **Relevance:** Directly relevant to East African pigs

### 2. Locally Adapted Kenyan Pigs (2021)
- **Population:** Kenyan local pigs
- **Loci:** SLA-1, SLA-2, DQB1
- **Data:** Allele diversity
- **Source:** 2021 study
- **Relevance:** Directly relevant

### 3. Cameroon Pig Study
- **Population:** Local, hybrid, exotic African pigs
- **Loci:** SLA-1, SLA-2
- **Data:** Allele frequencies
- **Relevance:** African pig populations
