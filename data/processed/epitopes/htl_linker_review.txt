HTL LINKER BIOLOGY — EVIDENCE REVIEW
================================================================================

1. GPGPG Linker (Gly-Pro-Gly-Pro-Gly)
   - Most commonly used HTL linker
   - Flexible, glycine-proline-rich sequence
   - Maintains structural independence of HTL epitopes
   - Important for MHC-II binding (HTL epitopes bind as 15-mers)

2. Alternative HTL Linkers
   - GGGGS — flexible, may not provide enough spatial separation
   - GGS — shorter, less separation
   - GPGPG is preferred for HTL constructs

3. MHC-II Processing
   - HTL epitopes are 15-mers (your HTLs are 15-mers)
   - MHC-II binding requires flexible presentation
   - Linker should not interfere with the 15-mer binding core

4. Junctional Peptide Risk
   - New 15-mer sequences formed at linker-epitope junctions
   - May create unintended MHC-II binding peptides
   - Should be assessed computationally

5. Specific Recommendations for Your Panel:
   - 3 HTL candidates (p54, pp220, p30) — all 15-mers
   - GPGPG is appropriate, but junctional 15-mers should be checked
   - Consider whether linkers create new 9-mer CTL epitopes
