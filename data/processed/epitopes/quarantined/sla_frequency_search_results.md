#### Data Gaps

### Conclusion
SLA allele frequency data for East African pig populations is **not available** at the scale needed for population coverage calculation.

### Recommended Approach
1. Use allele breadth (6/6 represented alleles) as the primary metric
2. Document population coverage as a limitation
3. Report: "The selected CTL panel recognized all six SLA alleles represented in the curated reference panel"
4. Do NOT claim population coverage percentages
