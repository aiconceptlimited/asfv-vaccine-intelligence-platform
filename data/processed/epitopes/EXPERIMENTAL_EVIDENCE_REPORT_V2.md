   - SLA associations: SLA-104:01 and SLA-102:01.

2. **2022 Study.** "Computational prediction of pp220 CTL epitopes." *FAO AGRIS*.
   - Predicted SLA-I peptides: SQWDLVQKF, SAMEVLHEL, YTDIVQKKY, FIINIRSFK, RLDRKHILM.
   - YTDIVQKKY: SLA-1*0401, MHC IC50 53 nM.
   - RLDRKHILM: SLA-1*0401, MHC IC50 89.1 nM.

3. **2023 Study.** "CP312R linear antigenic determinants."
   - Identified 78-87: DEEVIRMNAE and 122-131: KNEQGEEIYP.
   - Method: Monoclonal antibodies, peptide scanning, Western blot, IFA.

4. **2024 Study.** "CP312R B-cell epitope." *Veterinary Microbiology*.
   - Identified 72-83: TIPPSTDEEVIR.

   - Identified 117-125: SSFETLFEQ as a highly conserved immunodominant p30 epitope.
   - Method: Swine-derived scFv, blocking ELISA.

   - Identified 116-125: TSSFETLFEQ recognized by Q4-1F10B7.
