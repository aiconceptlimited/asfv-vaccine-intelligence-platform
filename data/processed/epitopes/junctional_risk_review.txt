JUNCTIONAL EPITOPE RISK — EVIDENCE REVIEW
================================================================================

1. What Are Junctional Epitopes?
   - New sequences formed at linker-epitope junctions
   - Can create unintended CTL or HTL epitopes
   - May cause off-target immune responses

2. How to Assess Junctional Epitopes
   - Extract all junctional peptides (linker + flanking epitope residues)
   - Predict MHC-I and MHC-II binding
   - Check against self/host proteome for off-target risk

3. Specific Recommendations for Your Panel:
   - 11 candidates = 10 junctions
   - CTL junctions: epitope-AAY-epitope
   - HTL junctions: epitope-GPGPG-epitope
   - HTL-CTL junctions: epitope-GPGPG-AAY-epitope (or similar)
   - Each junction creates new peptide sequences
   - All junctional peptides should be assessed
