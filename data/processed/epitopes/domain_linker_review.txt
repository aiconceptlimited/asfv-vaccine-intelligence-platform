DOMAIN/ADJUVANT SEPARATION — EVIDENCE REVIEW
================================================================================

1. EAAAK Linker (Glu-Ala-Ala-Ala-Lys)
   - Rigid α-helical spacer
   - Used to separate functional domains
   - Well-established in protein engineering

2. When EAAAK is Justified
   - When an adjuvant/domain requires spatial separation
   - When structural rigidity is needed
   - Not always necessary for simple epitope constructs

3. Orientation of Adjuvant Relative to Epitopes
   - N-terminal vs C-terminal placement
   - Affects processing and presentation
   - Should be decided based on biology

4. Specific Recommendations for Your Panel:
   - Consider if an adjuvant is being used
   - If not, EAAAK may not be necessary
   - Simpler architecture may be preferred
