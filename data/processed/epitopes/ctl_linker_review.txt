CTL LINKER BIOLOGY — EVIDENCE REVIEW
================================================================================

1. AAY Linker (Ala-Ala-Tyr)
   - Most commonly used CTL linker in multi-epitope vaccines
   - Recognized by proteasome for cleavage
   - Creates defined junction between CTL epitopes
   - Evidence: Widely cited in MEV literature

2. Alternative CTL Linkers
   - AAA (Ala-Ala-Ala) — simpler, less defined cleavage
   - GG (Gly-Gly) — flexible, may affect processing
   - AAY is preferred for defined cleavage

3. Proteasomal Processing
   - CTL epitopes require proteasomal cleavage for MHC-I presentation
   - Linker composition affects cleavage efficiency
   - AAY is recognized as a proteasomal cleavage site

4. Junctional Peptide Risk
   - New peptides formed at linker-epitope junctions
   - May create unintended epitopes
   - Should be assessed computationally

5. Specific Recommendations for Your Panel:
   - 8 CTL candidates (7 pp220, 1 p30)
   - AAY is appropriate, but junctional peptides should be checked
   - Consider adjacent epitope compatibility
