DATASET DEFINITIONS — ML TRAINING
=================================

CTL Dataset:
- Positive: Experimentally supported peptide-SLA-I epitopes
  - Source: IEDB, published literature
  - Criteria: SLA-I binding assay, T-cell assay, ELISpot
  - Allele: SLA-1, SLA-2, SLA-3
- Negative: Random peptides from ASFV proteins
  - Criteria: Not predicted as epitopes, no literature support
  - Length: 8-11 aa

HTL Dataset:
- Positive: Experimentally supported peptide-SLA-II epitopes
  - Source: IEDB, published literature
  - Criteria: SLA-II binding assay, T-cell assay
  - Allele: SLA-DRB1, SLA-DQA, SLA-DQB1
- Negative: Random peptides from ASFV proteins
  - Criteria: Not predicted as epitopes, no literature support
  - Length: 15 aa

B-cell Dataset:
- Positive: Experimentally supported linear B-cell epitopes
  - Source: IEDB, published literature
  - Criteria: Antibody binding, ELISA, peptide microarray
- Negative: Random peptides from ASFV proteins
  - Criteria: No antibody binding evidence
  - Length: 15 aa

Exclusion Criteria for All:
- Ambiguous sequence (X, B, Z, J)
- Length < 8 aa or > 15 aa
- Unresolved allele/assay
- Duplicates
- ASFV candidate peptides (11 core + 4 exploratory)
